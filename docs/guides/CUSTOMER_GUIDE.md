# Customer guide

How to print a PDF at a Kaggaz shop in Mandya and collect it. You need a phone or computer with a
browser. No app to install.

> **Demo notice.** In the prototype every page shows an amber bar: **DEMO PAYMENT — NO MONEY
> CHARGED**. Payments are simulated. No money leaves your account and no real refund is made.

## 1. Choose a shop

1. Open the Kaggaz website. The home page lists the print shops.
2. Each shop shows its address, whether it is open, and what it can print (B/W or colour,
   single- or double-sided).
3. Tap the shop where you want to collect your prints. A shop marked **Shop unavailable** is
   closed or under maintenance; pick another one or come back later.

## 2. Upload your PDF

1. On **New order**, tap **Choose a PDF to print** (or drag the file onto it).
2. Only PDF files work. The limit is shown on the page (default 25 MB and 300 pages).
3. Kaggaz checks the file itself, not just its name. You see **Checking your document…** and then
   the page count, e.g. _"12 pages · ready to print"_.
4. Password-protected or damaged PDFs are refused. Remove the password, save a fresh PDF and try
   again.

Tip: to print a Word file or photo, first save it as PDF on your phone or computer
("Print → Save as PDF").

## 3. Choose print options

| Option | What it means                                                                                                                    |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Copies | How many full copies of the selected pages (up to 20).                                                                           |
| Colour | **B/W** or **Colour**. Colour is greyed out at shops without a colour printer.                                                   |
| Sides  | **Single-sided** or **Double-sided**. Greyed out ("Double-sided not available here") when the shop's printer can't do it itself. |
| Pages  | Leave empty for all pages, or type a range like `1-3, 5`.                                                                        |
| Paper  | Always A4.                                                                                                                       |

## 4. Check the price

- The price is calculated by Kaggaz's server from the shop's rates. It updates each time you change
  an option.
- You pay **per printed side**: pages × copies × rate. Example: 3 pages × 2 copies = 6 sides; at
  ₹2 per side that is ₹12.
- Some shops have a **minimum charge**. If your order is smaller, the minimum is shown and charged.
- The pickup slip the shop prints with your order is free.
- If the shop changes its prices while you are on the page, Kaggaz shows the new price before you
  pay. You are never charged a price you didn't see.

## 5. Pay

1. Tap **Pay**.
2. In the demo, the payment is simulated and the page says _"Demo payment — no money was
   charged"_.
3. If payment fails you see **Payment didn't go through** and _"No money was taken. You can try
   again."_ Tap pay again. Pressing Pay twice never charges twice.
4. If the payment is still being confirmed, wait a moment; the page checks again by itself.
5. Before you pay you can **Cancel order**. Unpaid orders expire after 24 hours.

The shop only gets your order after Kaggaz has confirmed the payment with the payment provider.

## 6. Your pickup number

As soon as payment is confirmed, the order page shows your **pickup number with the date**, in
large type, for example:

> **#7 · 7 Nov**

- Each shop counts its paid orders from **#1** every day (the count restarts at midnight, India
  time). The date stops yesterday's #7 being mixed up with today's.
- The same number is printed in large type on the **pickup slip** on top of your pages.
- The number never changes for your order, even if the shop has to reprint it.

## 7. Track your order

The order page updates by itself. You can also reload it any time.

| Status shown         | What is happening                                                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Waiting for the shop | Paid. The shop will accept it shortly (some shops accept automatically). |
| In the print queue   | Accepted; it prints in turn.                                             |
| Printing             | Your pages are printing now.                                             |
| Being checked        | The shop is checking the printer. Nothing for you to do.                 |
| Ready for pickup     | Printing finished. Go to the shop.                                       |
| Rejected             | The shop couldn't print it. The reason is shown; you get a refund.       |
| Refund in progress   | Your refund is on its way.                                               |
| Refunded             | The refund is done.                                                      |

**Tracking link.** The order page has a **Tracking link** with a **Copy** button. Save it (or keep
the browser tab). Anyone with this link can see the order and its pickup number, so share it only
with someone collecting for you.

## 8. Collect your prints

1. Go to the shop when the status is **Ready for pickup**.
2. Say your number, e.g. _"Copies for number 7, please"_, and show the order page with **#7 · 7 Nov**.
3. The shopkeeper finds your packet by the slip on top and hands it over. In some shops you may
   take it from the output tray yourself — check the slip shows your number and today's date.
4. There is no code to type and nothing to sign.

Your files are deleted from Kaggaz's storage automatically after a short time (PRD §14.3).

## 9. Sign in with your mobile number (optional)

You can order without an account. Sign in to see all your orders in one place.

1. Tap **Sign in** (top right).
2. Enter your 10-digit Indian mobile number.
3. Enter the 6-digit code you receive. It expires in 5 minutes. Too many wrong tries locks sign-in
   for a few minutes.
4. After signing in, **My orders** lists your orders, newest first. Orders placed earlier with the
   same verified number are added to your list.

In the demo no SMS is sent: the page says _"Demo: no SMS is sent. The code appears in the admin
demo page (OTP inbox)."_ Ask the person running the demo for the code.

## 10. Refunds

You are refunded automatically when:

- the shop **rejects** your paid order (for example the file can't be printed, the printer is out
  of ink, or the shop is closing), or
- the shop marks a failed print as **failed** instead of reprinting it.

The order page shows the reason and the refund status (**Refund in progress** → **Refunded**).
You don't need to ask. In the demo the page says **"Demo refund — no money moved"**.

If something looks wrong (status stuck for a long time, wrong prints, missing pages), go to the
shop with your order page open, or contact Kaggaz support with your order reference
(`KGZ-XXXXXX`, shown at the top of the order page).
